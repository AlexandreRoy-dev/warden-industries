SEC-02 / Sector

Cybersecurity for private-sector businesses.

Practical security for companies that depend on their networks, their data and their buildings: a clear picture of your risks, then the controls and the plan to manage them.

Overview

Private sector

Most businesses do not need a list of every possible threat. They need to know where they are exposed, what to fix first and what to do if something goes wrong. We assess your networks, cloud services, remote access and building systems, and turn what we find into a prioritized plan.

We then help you put it in place: hardened systems, segmented networks, monitoring and backups you can rely on, and staff who know what to do. Engagements are scaled to the business, from a single office to several sites.

Practical security policies and staff awareness training.
