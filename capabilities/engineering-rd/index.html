CAP-04 / Capability

Secure engineering and R&D for technology that has to hold up.

We develop robust technologies for high value targets of cyber attackers. When a device, a control system or a piece of software has to resist determined attackers, we help design it that way and test it before it is deployed.

Overview

Secure Engineering and R&D

Security is far cheaper to design in than to add later. We work with your engineers from the requirement stage: who would attack the system, what they would want, and which design choices make that hard. The result is a set of security requirements and an architecture your team can build to.

We then help prove it: design and configuration reviews, firmware and update mechanisms, and lab testing of devices and systems against the agreed requirements, with findings your developers can act on.

Scope of work

What we deliver

01

Threat modelling

Who could attack the system, how and why, turned into clear security requirements.

02

Secure system design

Architecture, trust boundaries and data flows designed to limit what an attacker can reach.

03

Embedded and device security

Secure boot, signed updates, protected keys and locked debug access for connected equipment.

04

Security testing

Lab testing of devices, firmware and systems against the agreed requirements.

05

Prototypes and proofs of concept

Short iterations to prove that a security approach works before it is built at scale.

06

Documentation and handover

Design records, test results and guidance your team can maintain.

Process

How a program runs

  1. 01

    Requirement

    Your operations, constraints and risks, captured as clear and measurable requirements.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    What is actually there: assets, connections, access and procedures, reviewed with your team.

  3. 03

    Design review

    Architecture, priorities and trade-offs, reviewed with you before anything is changed or built.

  4. 04

    Implementation and testing

    Changes put in place in planned windows and tested against the agreed requirements.

  5. 05

    Handover and support

    Documentation, training for your team and ongoing support.
